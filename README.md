# edgeworth-competitor-research
Python-based competitor research and web scraping automation for trading and investment platforms.

 ## Project Overview
This project involved conducting competitor research for EDGEWORTH, focusing on trading platforms, copy-trading services, and investment companies operating across different markets.

The main objective was to identify relevant competitors, investigate their services and business models, and automate the collection of publicly available information to make competitor analysis more efficient and structured.

# Project Workflow

1. Manual Competitor Research

Started by manually researching and identifying companies operating in the trading, investment, and copy-trading industries. Reviewed search results, company websites, and available industry information to identify relevant competitors for further analysis.

2. Competitor Selection

Evaluated the identified companies and shortlisted relevant platforms based on their services, business models, copy-trading capabilities, and relevance to the research objectives.

3. Website Identification and Research Planning

Located the official websites of selected companies and identified relevant pages containing information about company profiles, products, pricing, trading features, regulations, and other business details.

4. Web Scraping Automation

Developed Python-based web scraping workflows using DrissionPage to navigate websites and BeautifulSoup to parse page content. Implemented automated crawling to discover relevant internal pages and extract publicly available information for competitor research.

5. Data Extraction and Structuring

Collected and organized relevant information across multiple research categories, including:

Company profiles and business models

Products, services, and platform features

Pricing, fees, and subscription plans

Minimum investment and deposit requirements

Copy-trading functionality and trading strategies

Risk management tools and trader performance information

Trader rankings and performance transparency

Regulatory information and licenses

Technology, integrations, and supported markets

Availability across Nigeria, Africa, and other regions

Public reviews, complaints, and partnerships

6. Data Organization and Export

Structured the extracted information into organized datasets for easier review and comparison. Used Pandas to process tabular data and supported export to CSV and Excel formats, along with crawl logs to track website crawling activity.

7. Competitor Analysis Support

Prepared the collected information to support competitor comparisons, identify differences in platform offerings, understand business models, and provide a structured foundation for further market research.
